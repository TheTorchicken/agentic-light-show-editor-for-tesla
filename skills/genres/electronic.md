# Electronic and bass music

EDM, dubstep, phonk, drum and bass, dance pop: songs built around builds, drops and a
steady kit. Learned from Grief and Tidal Wave.

## What the owner said

- "It should have every moment: strobes, fades and movement." Electronic songs give you all
  three; use all three.
- Tidal Wave's first draft was "way too calm for the first while... this song starts strong."
  Match the song's real opening energy. Don't default to a quiet intro because most songs
  have one.
- Grief went dark from one end of the car for long stretches. Every moment must read from
  the front alone and from the rear alone.

## How to choreograph it

1. **The drop is the show.** Make the last bar before it the darkest moment in the song
   (black, or a single accelerating strobe), then hit with everything: main beams, brake,
   reverse, screen at full, every accent, and a closure move landing on it (a window dance,
   mirrors folding). Windows are already down: the music only plays from the cabin.
2. **Builds accelerate.** Risers map to strobes that speed up (300 ms → 60 ms periods) and to
   the screen and accents brightening. Ping-pong front and rear, or left and right, so the
   build has motion, not just speed.
3. **Stutters and gaps are blackouts.** When the track chops (silence for 100-300 ms), cut
   every light, then burst back. The analysis lists silent gaps; use them exactly.
4. **One motif per section.** Drop 1, drop 2 and the final drop should each look different:
   e.g. front/rear call-and-response, then diagonal chases, then the whole car strobing.
   The critique flags neighboring sections that look alike.
5. **Kick, snare and hats get different lights.** Kick → brake or main beams; snare/clap → DRL
   or amber turns alternating sides; hats → side repeaters and plate. Keep the mapping
   consistent so the audience learns it.
6. **Breakdowns breathe.** Strip to one or two slow elements (DRL fading on 2 s ramps, the
   screen pulsing with the kick) at both ends of the car.
7. **The screen pulses with the groove** (kick on the beat, color on the off-beat) and goes to
   full white only at drops and peaks.

## Check before you finish

- The bar before each drop is visibly darker than the drop.
- Strobes appear at the peaks, not throughout.
- Neither end of the car is dark for more than a bar while music plays.
