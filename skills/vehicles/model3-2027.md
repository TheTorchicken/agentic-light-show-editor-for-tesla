# Model 3 (2027) technique

Profile: `profiles/model3-2027.json`. Everything here was measured on a real car.

## The palette

Per side, front: outer main beam, inner main beam, DRL strip, turn signal, side
repeater. Rear: turn (amber part of the C tail), tail (red C), plus shared brake,
reverse, rear fog and plate. Inside: five RGB accent segments forming one upside-down U around the cabin
(left rear, left front, dash center, right front, right rear), and the screen, which
sits below the center of the U, floods the cabin with light, and is the single
most important light in a show.

There is no signature light, no fogs, no aux park, no side markers and no light bars.
Don't spend channels on them.

## What each light is good for

| Light | Moves like | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Outer main beam | instant on/off, flashes as short as 20 ms | strobes, hard hits, kicks |
| Inner main beam | ramps (0.5 / 1 / 2 s on, 0.35 / 1 / 2 s off) | the big swell, the brightest sustained moment |
| DRL strip (ch 4) | ramps, outlines the lamp | vocals, melody, breathing, the "eyes" of the car |
| Front turn | slow ramps, amber, ~40 ms min flash | color accents, slow amber washes, side-to-side |
| Side repeater | instant on/off | hats, fast percussion, side punctuation |
| Rear turn | instant on/off, amber | rear call-and-response with the front turns |
| Tail (L/R) | instant on/off, red | bass, left-right rear alternation |
| Brake | instant on/off, both tails + high bar + low red | big rear hits; it's the loudest rear light |
| Reverse | instant on/off, white low | contrast hits at the rear |
| Plate | instant on/off | small hats or ticks |
| Interior accents (U) | full color and brightness, 5 segments | mood, sweeps along the U, side swaps, front/back answers |
| Screen | full color and brightness, the brightest light on the car | the lead instrument: color story, beat pulses, strobes at peaks, active in every section |

Ramp codes (measured, not the README): turn on 179 = slow 2 s, 204 = 1 s,
230 = fast 0.5 s; turn off 77 = slow 2 s, 51 = 1 s, 26 = fast 0.35 s. To get a fade
that doesn't complete, end the effect at least 100 ms before the ramp finishes.
Channels 5 and 6 of "Channel 4/5/6" drive the same DRL strip as channel 4: only
use channel 4 (7/8).

## Combinations that read well

- **Eyes open**: DRL ramp on over 2 s → inner main beam ramp on 0.5 s at the downbeat.
- **Blink**: outer main beam 80-150 ms flash on the hit, DRL stays on underneath.
- **Amber ping-pong**: front turn L, then rear turn R, then front turn R, then rear
  turn L, one per beat. It travels diagonally around the car.
- **Heartbeat**: brake on the kick, tails on the off-beat, 100-150 ms each.
- **Wash**: interior color fades with the pad while exterior stays dark.

## Dance moves (closures)

The car can move as well as light up. Use `s.closure(channel, t, "open" | "close" | "dance" | "stop", until)`.
The viewer animates them, and the validator enforces Tesla's per-show limits.

| Move | Channel | Takes | Limit per show | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mirror fold / unfold | 35 (L), 36 (R) | ~2 s | 20 each | a slow "bow"; fold both on a big hit, unfold on a release |
| Window down / up | 37 LF, 38 LR, 39 RF, 40 RR | ~4 s | 6 each | slow and dramatic; also lets the music out of the cabin |
| Window dance | 37-40 | oscillates while held | 6 each | a quick shimmy; works from closed |
| Charge port open / dance / close | 46 | ~2 s; dance keeps the door open and flashes the port LED rainbow | 3 total | a sparkle on the side; it must be open before it dances |
| Trunk open / dance / close | 41 | 14 s open, 4 s close; dance bobs it near fully open | 6 | too slow for rhythm; an opening or ending gesture, a bob at a peak once it's up |

How to use them:

- **Closures are choreography, not rhythm.** They are too slow to hit beats. Use them on section
  changes, drops and the ending, ideally starting a beat or two before the moment so the motion
  lands on it.
- **Mirrors are the best move.** Fast, symmetric and visible from front and side. Fold on the
  first drop, unfold on the breakdown, fold and unfold as a pair to "breathe" with a big swell.
  Staggering left and right by a beat reads as a nod.
- **Window dance** is the only fast move. Keep it to 2-4 bars at a peak. The cabin speakers are
  inside, so windows down also makes the show louder for the crowd.
- **Charge port**: open, then dance in the climax. Total dance time across all closures should
  stay around 30 s or less (thermal limits).
- **Never end a show with something open** unless you mean it; close or unfold before the last
  image, allowing for the movement time. A window dance leaves the glass wherever the swing
  stopped, so follow every dance with a close. The compiler warns if anything ends open.

Note: closures have not yet been tested on this car (the closures probe hasn't been filmed). The
timings above are from Tesla's documentation.

### Open vs dance

- **Open / close**: one move to the end position at the closure's speed. It completes even if the
  command returns to idle.
- **Dance**: oscillates only while held. Windows dance from any position, swinging about a third of their travel from where they
  are (down from closed, up from open) and back, about 1.1 s per swing; the trunk only once open (bobbing near fully open); the charge port
  only once open, and its door stays put while the LED flashes rainbow. Mirrors cannot dance.
  When the dance ends the closure stays where the swing stopped, so close it afterwards.
- The swing amplitudes are approximations until the closures probe is filmed.
